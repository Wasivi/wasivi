How We Work

It starts with a focused discovery phase

Before any design or development begins, we clarify the opportunity, the user, and what the first useful version actually needs to be. That clarity shapes everything that follows.

Work moves in phases, not one open-ended contract

Each phase has a defined scope and a clear deliverable. You approve the next phase because the last one earned it — not because a clock ran out.

I lead every engagement directly

The person scoping your project is the person building it. When a project needs additional research, technical, brand, or domain expertise, I bring in trusted specialists rather than keeping a large permanent bench.

AI accelerates the work, judgment directs it

AI speeds up exploration, research synthesis, and parts of implementation. Every product decision and every client-facing outcome is still guided and reviewed by a person before it goes out.

You end up owning something real

Whether the engagement is a focused sprint or a longer build, it ends in a clear handoff — a product direction, a prototype, or a deliverable your team can take forward.
