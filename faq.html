FAQ

GENERAL
What does WASIVI build? +
WASIVI helps founders and teams turn early ideas into clear, credible digital products. We shape MVPs, prototypes, launch experiences, AI-enabled concepts, and presentation stories that help people understand what to build next.
Can I start with something focused before committing to a larger build? +
Yes. Many engagements begin with an MVP Blueprint, product strategy session, presentation, or prototype. Start with the work that creates the most clarity now, then decide what comes next.
ENGAGEMENT
How does an engagement typically start? +
Most engagements begin with a focused discovery phase. We clarify the opportunity, the user, the first useful version, and the decisions needed before design or development begins.
What does a typical engagement look like? +
Work is organized into defined phases with a clear scope, tangible deliverables, and a decision point at the end of each phase. The next phase begins only when the work has earned it.
What's the typical timeline? +
A focused phase may take a few weeks. More involved product, prototype, or build work can extend over several phases, depending on complexity and the pace of decision-making.
INVESTMENT & HANDOFF
What's the investment for an engagement? +
It depends on the shape of the work. Focused engagements are scoped as fixed projects; larger product programs are priced phase by phase. You will know the scope, deliverables, and investment for a phase before it begins.
Who owns the work once it is complete? +
Once the agreed work is complete and final payment is received, you own the final project-specific deliverables. WASIVI retains ownership of its pre-existing methods, templates, tools, and reusable systems.
TEAM & AI
Who's actually doing the work? +
WASIVI is founder-led. I lead product direction, design, presentation storytelling, and the core build, and bring in trusted specialists when a project needs additional research, technical, brand, or domain expertise.
How is AI used in your process? +
AI helps accelerate exploration, research synthesis, prototyping, and selected implementation work. Human product judgment and design direction guide every decision, and every client-facing outcome is reviewed before delivery.
Can WASIVI work with my existing team or developer? +
Yes. WASIVI can lead a focused project independently or work alongside your internal team, agency, or developer. The goal is a clear handoff and a product direction your team can continue to build.
