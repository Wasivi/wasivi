# WASIVI — Product Requirements Document (MVP Stage)

**Prepared by:** Natalie Walker **Status:** MVP / Pre-launch **Last updated:** September 2026

---

## 1\. Overview

WASIVI is a founder-led AI product studio. It turns early-stage ideas into working prototypes, MVPs, and presentation-ready product concepts for founders and teams who need to move from "idea" to "something a person can actually see and evaluate" quickly.

At this stage, WASIVI is itself an MVP: one founder, a small set of proven collaborators brought in per project, and a portfolio of six built products that demonstrate the studio's actual working method rather than a hypothetical one.

## 2\. Problem Statement

Founders and teams with an early idea typically face one of two bad options: spend months and significant budget on a full build before knowing if the idea holds up, or settle for a slide deck that doesn't give anyone — investors, customers, internal stakeholders — anything real to react to. The gap between "idea" and "something tangible" is where most early-stage work stalls.

## 3\. Target Customer

- Founders with an early-stage idea who need a working prototype or MVP to raise funding, pitch a partner, or validate direction before committing to a full build.  
- Teams inside larger organizations who need a fast, credible proof of concept to get internal buy-in.  
- Anyone who needs a presentation-quality product story — not just a working build, but a way to explain it clearly to a non-technical audience.

## 4\. What WASIVI Delivers

- **MVP Blueprints** — scoped definitions of what the first useful version of a product should be.  
- **Prototypes** — working, demonstrable builds, not static mockups.  
- **AI-enabled concepts** — products where AI is a core part of the value, not a bolt-on feature.  
- **Presentation and storytelling** — the narrative and visual materials that make a product legible to investors, customers, or internal stakeholders.

## 5\. Business Model

WASIVI is founder-led rather than team-based at this stage. Natalie leads every engagement's direction, design, and core build directly, and brings in specialists (technical, brand, research, or domain experts) as individual projects require, rather than carrying a permanent bench. Engagements are structured in defined phases with fixed scope and deliverables per phase — pricing is either fixed (for focused engagements) or scoped phase-by-phase (for larger programs), never open-ended.

## 6\. MVP Scope — What Exists Today

**Live/built:**

- Six demonstrated products: Hyperscale Data Center Intelligence, LinkUp, Work Clarity, Bloombase, SIGHTLINE, and METU (in progress) — serving as the studio's proof of working method, not client work-for-hire.  
- SIGHTLINE has a confirmed first real customer (Dr. Ravi Parikh), making it the studio's first validated engagement outcome.  
- A public-facing site (wasivi.com) presenting the studio, its founder story, its work, and how engagements work — covering Home, About, How We Work, FAQ, and Projects.

**Not yet built:**

- A public team/people page (not needed yet — studio is currently solo-plus-collaborators).  
- A careers page (no open roles at this stage).  
- A dedicated case-study format per project (current Projects page uses short narrative summaries, not full case studies).  
- Formal client testimonials or a case-study-driven proof section.

## 7\. Differentiation

Natalie's path to WASIVI isn't a standard technical-founder story: twenty years running an international embroidery design and development studio (Walker Sinclair Visuals, with offices in China and New York, work landing in Vogue and the Met's Costume Institute Library), followed by running research and vetting for 246 boards and commissions at the NYC Mayor's Office of Appointments, where she began applying AI to that work as early as 2022\. The throughline across all three chapters is the same: tracking how an idea or a candidate moves through a complex system, spotting risk and failure points early, and making the outcome legible to whoever has to make a decision about it. That's the same skill WASIVI sells today, just applied to product development instead of embroidery production or civic appointments.

## 8\. Success Metrics for This Stage

- Site launches and is publicly reachable at wasivi.com.  
- At least one inbound engagement inquiry generated through the site itself.  
- SIGHTLINE's engagement with Dr. Ravi Parikh converts into a documented case study or testimonial.  
- Substack (read.wasivi.com) begins publishing on a regular cadence, reinforcing the studio's point of view.

## 9\. Open Questions / Risks

- Whether a solo-founder-plus-collaborators model scales past a certain number of simultaneous engagements without a more formal team page or structure.  
- Whether the current six-project portfolio needs a seventh or eighth entry before launch, or whether six is sufficient proof of range.  
- Pricing transparency — FAQ currently states pricing is phase-by-phase and fixed for focused work, but no public pricing anchor exists yet (e.g., a starting price for the smallest engagement type).

## 10\. Roadmap (Near-Term)

1. Launch wasivi.com via GitHub \+ Netlify, connected to the wasivi.com domain.  
2. Publish first Substack piece.  
3. Convert the SIGHTLINE engagement into a public case study or testimonial.  
4. Revisit whether People/Careers/News pages are warranted once there's real content for them.

## 11\. Out of Scope (For Now)

- Hiring or a formal team structure.  
- A self-serve product or productized offering — all engagements remain custom and founder-led at this stage.  
- Paid marketing or lead generation beyond the site and Substack.
